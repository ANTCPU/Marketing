## What the hub is
A single page that runs inside the system — the marketing counterpart to the launcher — where the whole operation is visible at a glance: what's posting, where, what's approved, what's working, and who it's for.

## What it must hold (from lived memory — this is the meta gather)

1. **The Rhythm** — the weekly beats: Mon + Wed big posting days, Tue + Thu light, Fri decisions. Morning posting round (fire-and-go from night-before approval), Circle Trigger 1 (~7:30 AM, elevate the circle), Trigger 2 (~3:30 PM, reply to every genuine comment). The comment-wave rhythm governs the day: post on ALL pages first in the early round, then read the comments — a wave of comments shows where the action already is, and we flood that timeliness with replies. We are the follow. Quiet hours in view.
2. **Surfaces inventory** — every place we post, in its own lane:
   - Map of Pi: 10 Facebook pages + the group (36k+); Map of Pi identity ONLY (green pin, gold π) — never ANTCPU branding. Group-share pattern: the MAIN page's post is shared into the group once, by Joseph's own tap — the other pages never share the same post into the group.
   - ANTCPU: Facebook page, secondary pages (Turtle, Voiceover Masterpieces, Cash Out Daily, Fill in the Blank), Threads.
   - Agent pages (always-on campaign layer): Bluesky, Mastodon, Tumblr, Pinterest, Nostr.
   - ABU Marketplace: page + group.
3. **The approval flow** — drafts staged → Joseph's tap → fire. Plan prepped the day before, approved the night before. Nothing publishes without his explicit green light. One tap at a time.
4. **Campaigns & series** — thankful series (MAC Oct 29 rerun; buyers Oct 28), Small Ball Big Wins (World Series run), event drumbeats (the Mr Ben 4-beat model: announce → story → reminder → day-of — live example TODAY, Fri Oct 23: Mr Ben Facebook Live 11 AM ET, then World Series Game 1 at 8 PM), sports rotation (baseball → golf → basketball thread), developer-journey series. **Profile Elevation by ANTCPU** — the flagship monthly service (picture, bio, growth); the presell posture: sell the concept and let the interested reveal themselves — never ask for interest, no keyword CTA ("a no response is a response"). **The cohort→arena flywheel** — October's cohort closed Oct 22 (33 challengers ≈ a third of the arena's 95 ads); November is Cohort 4, December Cohort 5, registration at antcpu.io/apply — every cohort feeds the arena, and the hub tracks that beat.
